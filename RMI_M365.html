A self-paced Microsoft 365 course for RMI government employees, combining short lectures with practical Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint, Forms, Planner, and cybersecurity activities.

Republic of the Marshall Islands Government Theme

This version is tailored for Republic of the Marshall Islands government employees. Activities use real Microsoft 365 practice labs so learners can complete them directly in their RMI work Microsoft 365 account while following public service communication and information security expectations.

  • Audience: RMI national and local government employees using Microsoft 365 for daily public service work.
  • Practice focus: Ministry communication, inter-agency meetings, citizen correspondence, secure document sharing, and cyber-safe workflows.
  • Outcome: Employees can work confidently in Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Office apps while protecting government information.
