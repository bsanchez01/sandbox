🎓 SONIS Student Portal — How to Use It

SONIS is your official Student Information System. Here's how to log in and navigate the key features.

🔐 How to Log In

1

Go to SONIS

Open your browser and visit sonis.cmi.edu — or access it from your MyPortal SSO dashboard.

2

Click Login → Student

Click the "Login" button on the homepage and select "Student" from the options.

3

Enter Your ID & PIN

Type in your Student ID and PIN, then click the "Login" button. Contact the IT Department if you need your login details.

4

You're In!

A successful login takes you to your biographical information page — your home base in SONIS.

📋 What You Can Do in SONIS

📅
View Your Schedule

Go to Academics → Schedule to see your enrolled courses, meeting times, and room locations. You can also view grades from previous semesters.

📝
Register for Courses

Go to Academics → Registration to enroll in courses for the upcoming semester. The system checks prerequisites automatically.

📊
Check Your Grades

View Midterm and Final grades from your Schedule page. Change the School Year and Semester to view past results.

💰
Billing Information

During registration, SONIS shows your anticipated billing based on selected courses. Note: this is not final until registration is complete.

🚫
Prerequisite Alerts

If you try to register for a course you haven't qualified for, SONIS will notify you of the required prerequisite course to take first.

✅
Complete Registration

After selecting your courses, click "Continue Registration Process" then "Complete Registration" to finalize your enrollment.

📧 Gmail Guide & Email Etiquette

Your CMI student email is @cmistudent.com — learn how to use it effectively and professionally.

1

Open Gmail

Log in at myportal.cmi.edu and click the Gmail icon. Your CMI email address ends in @cmistudent.com.

2

Click "Compose"

Click the big + Compose button in the top-left corner. A new message window will pop up in the bottom-right of your screen.

3

Fill in the "To" Field

Type the recipient's email address in the To field. For instructors, use their @cmi.edu address (e.g. instructor@cmi.edu). For classmates, use their @cmistudent.com address.

4

Add CC or BCC (Optional)

CC (Carbon Copy) sends a copy to others visibly. BCC (Blind CC) sends a copy without other recipients seeing it. Click "CC" or "BCC" next to the To field to add them.

5

Write a Clear Subject Line

Click the Subject field and type a brief, descriptive subject. Example: "Question about ICS 101 Assignment 2" — not just "Question" or "Hi".

6

Write Your Message

Click the large white area below the subject and type your message. Start with a greeting (e.g. "Dear Professor Smith,"), write your message clearly, and end with a sign-off (e.g. "Respectfully, [Your Name]").

7

Attach Files (If Needed)

Click the 📎 paperclip icon at the bottom of the compose window to attach files such as assignments or photos. You can also drag and drop files directly into the message.

8

Review & Send

Read your message once more to check for spelling and tone. When ready, click the blue Send button. A confirmation "Message sent" will appear at the bottom of your screen.

EXAMPLE OF A GOOD STUDENT EMAIL
To: instructor@cmi.edu
Subject: Question About ICS 101 Assignment 2 – [Your Name]
Dear Professor [Last Name],

I hope you are doing well. I am writing to ask for clarification on Assignment 2 for ICS 101. Specifically, I am unsure whether we need to submit both the written report and the diagram, or just the written report.

I would greatly appreciate your guidance. Thank you for your time.

Respectfully,
[Your Full Name]
Student ID: 2024-XXXX
ICS 101 – Section A
✅ Use a Professional Subject Line

Always include a clear, specific subject. Vague subjects like "Help" or "Question" may be ignored or delayed.

✅ Use a Proper Greeting

Start with "Dear Professor [Name]," or "Good morning, [Name]," — never start with "Hey" or no greeting at all.

✅ Identify Yourself

Always state your full name, Student ID, and which class you're in — instructors may have many students and won't always recognize your email.

✅ Be Clear and Concise

Get to your point quickly. State your question or concern in 2–4 sentences. Avoid very long emails when a short one will do.

✅ Use a Professional Sign-Off

End with "Respectfully," "Sincerely," or "Thank you," followed by your full name. Never just send a message with no sign-off.

✅ Check Before You Send

Re-read your email for spelling, grammar, and tone. A poorly written email reflects on your professionalism as a student.

❌ Don't Use All Caps

Writing in ALL CAPS comes across as shouting. Use normal capitalization throughout your message.

❌ Don't Use Slang or Emojis

Keep academic emails formal. Avoid texting language like "u," "lol," "gonna," or emoji — even if you're on good terms with the instructor.

❌ Don't Reply All Unnecessarily

If an instructor emails the whole class, only use "Reply All" if your response is relevant to everyone. Otherwise, reply only to the sender.

⏰ Respond Within 24–48 Hours

Check your CMI email daily. Instructors and the college send important notices — a missed email is not an accepted excuse for missed deadlines.

💡 Smart Tips for CMI Students

Keep these in mind throughout your time at CMI.

🔐 Keep Your Password Strong

Use uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never share your password — not even with IT staff.

💾 Always Back Up Your Work

Save to Google Drive or OneDrive — never only on a lab computer. Lab files may be wiped after sessions.

📧 Check Your Email Daily

Important announcements come to your @cmistudent.com email. Don't miss deadlines or class changes!

🔒 Log Out of Shared Computers

Always log out of all accounts after using lab computers to protect your student information.
