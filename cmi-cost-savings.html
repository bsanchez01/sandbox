Overview

The College of the Marshall Islands completed a full review of its technology systems, internet connections, software licenses, and cloud services. By removing unused licenses, switching to better-value providers, and modernizing infrastructure, CMI achieved significant annual cost reductions while improving the quality of services for students, faculty, and staff — including remote outer-island campuses.
