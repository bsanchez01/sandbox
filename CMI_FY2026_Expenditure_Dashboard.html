Assumptions & method — stated explicitly

  • Scope: transactions where the Fund code is 1602, 1613, 1711 or 1024 — 5,321 of the ledger's 22,804 rows. No other filters applied.
  • Net expenditure = Debit − Credit on every line; credits are treated as expenditure reductions/reversals, never as revenue.
  • Quarters are assigned from the PostTo date on CMI's October–September fiscal calendar. Q3 covers April 1 – June 25, 2026 only.
  • Category groups are inferred from GL account numbers and titles; the mapping is shown on the By Category tab.
  • Fund descriptions (Physical Plant, Continuing Education, Ebeye Center, academic salaries) are inferred from each fund's dominant department, as the extract carries codes only; Finance should substitute official fund titles before publication.
  • Vendor listing excludes payroll (TransSource PRS, $1,999,473.03 net in scope) and shows all 95 remaining payees, including negative-net correction entries.
  • In-scope extract parsed cleanly: 0 unreadable dates or amounts, 0 rows outside the FY2026 window, 0 blank payees.
