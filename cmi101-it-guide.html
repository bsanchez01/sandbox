📋 IT Acceptable Use Policy

✅ I will only use CMI systems for academic and educational purposes.

💡 Smart Tips for CMI Students

Keep these in mind throughout your time at CMI.

🔐 Keep Your Password Strong

Use a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never share your password with anyone — not even IT staff.

💾 Always Back Up Your Work

Save assignments to Google Drive or OneDrive — never only on a lab computer. Lab files may be wiped after sessions.

📧 Check Your Email Daily

Important announcements from instructors and CMI administration come via your @cmi.edu email. Don't miss deadlines!

🔒 Log Out of Shared Computers

Always log out of all accounts when using lab computers. Protect your student information from others.

📱 Use M365 Apps on Your Phone

Download the free Microsoft 365 mobile apps (Word, Teams, OneDrive) on your smartphone using your CMI credentials.

🎫 Submit a Ticket — Don't Wait

If something isn't working, submit a Help Desk ticket right away. Early tickets get faster resolutions.
