Key findings for Regents and Ministers

Plain-language highlights. Every figure is computed directly from the general ledger extract; nothing is estimated.

Monthly spending pattern

June reflects postings through June 25 only — one to two pay periods are missing from it. Peaks in October, March and May follow the payroll calendar (three pay dates in those months) plus fiscal-year-opening and vehicle purchases.

Where the money goes

Personnel & benefits alone account for 95 cents of every dollar in this scope — these are salary-driven funds.

Assumptions & method — stated explicitly

  • Scope interpretation: codes 1602, 1613, 1711 and 1024 are fund codes; 5411 is not a fund or GL account in this file — it is the department code for Student & Community Services, so all Dept 5411 activity is included (which brings $340,165.26 from Fund 0 and $608.51 from Fund 1050 into scope). MCST is excluded; verification showed zero MCST rows inside this scope regardless.
  • Net expenditure = Debit − Credit on every line; credits are treated as expenditure reductions/reversals, never as revenue.
  • Quarters are assigned from the PostTo date on CMI's October–September fiscal calendar. Q3 covers April 1 – June 25, 2026 only.
  • Category groups are inferred from GL account numbers and titles; the mapping is shown on the By Category tab.
  • Vendor rankings exclude payroll (TransSource PRS, $2,305,609.36 net in scope).
  • In-scope extract parsed cleanly: 6,761 rows; 0 unreadable dates or amounts, 0 rows outside the FY2026 window, 0 blank payees.
