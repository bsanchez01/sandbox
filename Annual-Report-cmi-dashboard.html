President's Message & Year at a Glance

2024

Message from President David A. Newnham — GO NAVIGATORS!

In 2024, despite challenges, CMI moved forward in its Mission to provide access to quality higher and further educational services. Three new Wapepe plans were approved addressing Equitable Student Success, Community Engagement, and Climate Change. The CMI Childcare Center was funded through the Asian Development Bank WYSER project. The college completed its 2021 audit in September — a key step towards restoring trust in its financial integrity.

Key Milestones 2024

⚓

Jela Meto Maritime Training Center Opened

Named at Uliga Dock July 2024. First cohort of 16 students began sea internships with MISC and World Bank ESSP.

🏗

CMI Childcare Center Funded

$1.5M matching fund approved with ADB WYSER Project — supports student parents and addresses gender gap in persistence.

🌿

First Agroforestry Graduate

AS in Agroforestry Education (introduced 2023) had its first completer in Fall 2024.

📝

2021 Audit Completed

Completed September — critical step toward restoring financial integrity and avoiding future reporting delays.

🎓

Wotje Center First College Graduate

Wotje Center honored its first-ever college-level graduate at the 2024 graduation ceremony.

🏔

Jaluit Graduation Returns

First graduation since 2020 — honoring 6 ABE and 3 college graduates.

🔧

First Advanced TVET Boot Camp

Advanced trades boot camp on Majuro and Ebeye — Automotive, Welding, Electrical. 75 total trainees.

🍀

Santo First ABE Graduation

CMI Santo Sub-Center held its first-ever ABE graduation ceremony in Summer 2024.
