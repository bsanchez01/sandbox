📋 Background & Strategy

During the 2025 fiscal year, CMI spent over $243,900 on Representation alone. The goal moving forward is to reduce this amount to $125,000 by holding more events on the CMI campus and tightly controlling spending on food.

FY2025 Actual$243,900+
New Target$125,000
🏛️ On-Campus Event Hosting

Over the past few years, it has been the practice to hold most college retreats and faculty in-service events at offsite locations. This typically includes six events per year with a facilities fee of $700 for each event.

Annual Savings from On-Campus Hosting
$4,200
6 events × $700 facility fee saved

Holding these events on CMI property will save $4,200 per year by eliminating facility rental fees entirely.

📝 Office of the President Memorandum

For the remainder of savings, the Office of the President will issue a memorandum limiting food spending to the following approved categories:

🎓 Student events
🤝 Events involving external stakeholders
🕐 Meetings & workshops during meal periods (lunch hour or after 5 PM)

📌 Budget Manager Directives

The memo will also direct all budget managers to ensure that they are working with less expensive vendors and to carefully limit the amount of food ordered.

All e-requisitions involving requests for food should include the expected number of participants in the event. This ensures proportionate ordering and accountability across all departments.

IT Systems Cost Savings

CMI conducted a comprehensive review of all technology subscriptions, internet services, and cloud platforms — eliminating waste, renegotiating contracts, and transitioning to smarter solutions.

Energy & Solar Infrastructure Savings

CMI is modernizing its energy systems through solar PV renewal and strategic energy management, reducing dependence on costly fuel imports and achieving long-term financial resilience.

☀️ Solar Project Note: The 119 kWp solar PV modernization began in March 2026 with support from NEO. Marine-grade, salt-spray resistant components are being deployed. The $1.7M figure represents cumulative savings over 20 years at $85,680/year.
Academic Program Cost Savings

Restructuring academic delivery — from consolidating ABE/AHS course offerings to transitioning from physical textbooks to eBooks — generates significant recurring savings while improving student access and outcomes.

Fuel & Energy Shortage Crisis Plan

CMI has a phased response plan for fuel and energy emergencies. The plan protects institutional operations while minimizing costs during any crisis — from rising prices to declared disasters.

The CMI Fuel & Energy Shortage Crisis Plan outlines institutional responses across three crisis phases (Blue, Orange, Red), plus a recovery phase (Green). Each phase defines responsibilities for all departments — from faculty and security to IT and Finance. The plan ensures continuity of education while protecting resources during emergencies.
