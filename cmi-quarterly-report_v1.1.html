04 · Strategic Goals

Strategic Goals — Progress & Status

CMI's five strategic pillars and current progress indicators · AY 2025–2026

Mission Statement

"The College of the Marshall Islands shall prioritize student success through access to education, cultural resources, research, and career guidance to prepare students to navigate their communities as global citizens."

College of the Marshall Islands
01
Academic Quality

Strengthen Academic Programs & Student Learning

AA in Marshallese Studies launched (world's first). Full CC in IT Support Level 1 launch. English Bridge Program achieved 24% direct credit placement. AS Nursing curriculum under comprehensive review. ASEN accreditation candidacy application in progress.

02
Enrollment & Access

Expand Access & Increase Enrollment

SP26 enrollment reached 1,033 (+6%). ABE serving 300+ learners across Uliga, Kwajalein, Wotje, Jaluit, and Kili. New sub-centers in Likiep and Ejit in development. Summer 2026 target: 500 students. First-Time student enrollment declined 38% — pipeline needs attention.

03
Workforce

Advance Workforce Development

TVET Boot Camp: 76 participants, 97% completion, 92% employment rate (2025). Maritime program graduated first CC-RFPNW/CC-RFPERW students — 100% workforce entry. New TVET building at Arrak opened April 15, 2026. Carpentry students building community water-catchment systems.

04
Accreditation

Maintain & Strengthen Accreditation

ACCJC Warning issued — 2022, 2023, 2024 audits required by November 1, 2026. ISER due August 1, 2027. Four committees formed (Mission & Effectiveness, Student Success, Infrastructure & Resources, Governance). Land Grant confirmed $100K for accreditation support. All-college retreat held April 15, 2026.

05
Financial Health

Ensure Financial Sustainability

Combined FY26 budget: $18.14M (Restricted $8.77M + Unrestricted $9.37M). Unrestricted utilization at 36.7% — significant budget available. Restricted student aid variance reflects federal pass-through activity. Pursuing Educational Talent Search Grant ($250K/yr) and other federal funding.

06
Leadership

Strengthen Institutional Leadership & Governance

Presidential hire and VPBAA search posted. Interim President in place. CMI Retreat held to align all-college priorities. Four ISER committees co-chaired by faculty and staff. ACCJC liaison visit conducted. Dean of WAVES position being filled for fall 2026.
