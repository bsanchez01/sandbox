Key findings for Regents and Ministers

Plain-language highlights. Every figure is computed directly from the general ledger extract; nothing is estimated.

Monthly spending pattern

June reflects postings through June 25 only. The January peak is the spring-semester Pell disbursement; the October peak is the fall disbursement plus fiscal-year-opening payments.

Where the money goes

Personnel and student aid together account for roughly three of every four dollars spent.

Assumptions & method — stated explicitly

  • Net expenditure = Debit − Credit on every line; credits are treated as expenditure reductions/reversals, never as revenue.
  • Quarters are assigned from the PostTo date on CMI's October–September fiscal calendar. Q3 covers April 1 – June 25, 2026 only (~93% of the quarter's calendar days).
  • Category groups (personnel, student aid, contractual, etc.) are inferred from GL account numbers and titles; the full account-to-group mapping is shown on the By Category tab for Finance to verify.
  • Fund descriptions are inferred from each fund's dominant department and account, because the extract carries fund codes only, not official fund names. Fund 0 is treated as the general/operating fund.
  • Vendor rankings exclude payroll (TransSource PRS, $5,121,396.66 net). Individuals appearing in the vendor list are contract-services payees, landlords, or reimbursement recipients paid through A/P — not employees on payroll.
  • The file parsed cleanly: 0 unreadable dates, 0 unreadable amounts, 0 rows outside the FY2026 window.
