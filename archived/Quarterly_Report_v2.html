Financial Performance Narrative

  • Liquidity: The report's historical liquidity ratio declined from 1.67 in FY2008 to 0.87 in FY2015, indicating tighter short-term financial capacity.
  • Revenue and expenditure trend: General Fund revenue increased through FY2014 before declining in FY2015, while expenditures remained above revenue in FY2015.
  • Net position: The trend shows deficits in FY2010–FY2012, surpluses in FY2013–FY2014, and a deficit again in FY2015.
  • FY2023–2024 budget balance: Total revenue and total expenditures are both reported at $11,211,840.
  • Cost structure: Personnel costs are the largest expenditure category at 62.78%, followed by other costs at 32.47% and non-operating costs at 4.75%.

Challenges & Recommendations

  • Instructor recruitment challenges in STEM and Marine Science.
  • Procurement delays affecting supplies and equipment availability.
  • Need for additional counseling staff and academic support personnel.
  • Expand infrastructure for flexible learning and IT systems.
  • Increase funding for professional development and student support.
