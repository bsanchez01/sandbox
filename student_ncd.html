Methodology and assumptions

  1. SONIS_ID is the only matching key. IDs are converted to text, trimmed, and uppercased before comparison. Students are never matched by name, email, RMI_SSN, or SIS_ID.
  2. A student is reported as Did Not Continue when their normalized SONIS_ID appears in the earlier semester’s file but is not found anywhere in the following semester’s file.
  3. A missing record means only that the student was not found in the following semester’s enrollment file. "Did not continue" does not automatically mean dropout. Possible reasons include graduation, stop-out, transfer, leave, withdrawal, or incomplete registration; no reason is assigned unless the source data explicitly provides one.
  4. Withdrawn students in the earlier semester (markers: Yes, Y, True, 1, Withdrawn, W) are kept in the Did Not Continue listing and flagged, but excluded from retention-eligible rates.
  5. Duplicate SONIS_IDs are reduced to one record per student per semester (the most complete row is kept). Blank SONIS_ID records are excluded from retention calculations and listed under Data Quality.
  6. RMI_SSN and BIRTHDATE are never displayed or exported by this dashboard.
